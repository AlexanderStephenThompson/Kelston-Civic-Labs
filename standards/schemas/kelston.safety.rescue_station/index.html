Rescue Station

kelston.safety.rescue_station v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like RSTN-000482

A located, equipped point a rescue crew launches from - a boathouse on the shore, a perch tower over the canopy, a shored tunnel head at the burrow tier. Stations make response coverage a thing on the map, not a promise in a drawer.
