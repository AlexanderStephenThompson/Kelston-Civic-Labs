School

kelston.education.school v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like SCHL-000482

An organization that teaches - an early-years den, the lyceum, a trade guild school, the open lifelong hall. Schools are organizations first; what this adds is which stages they teach, in which bands, and whether every learner can physically fit through the door and into a seat.
