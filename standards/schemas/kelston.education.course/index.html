Education & Learning

Course

kelston.education.course v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like CRSE-000482

One named offering at one school - a unit of teaching that citizens enroll in, with a stage, a band, and a tempo. The tempo matters: Kelston paces curricula by lifespan band, so a brief-band mouse and a venerable tortoise each get a whole education in a lifetime that fits it.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
school ref · school required The school that offers and timetables this course.
name text required The course's listed name, e.g. "Letters and Numbers II".
stage vocab · learning_stage required The learning stage this course belongs to.
offered_band vocab · shift_band required The shift band the course meets in, so cohorts share their waking hours.
paced_for_lifespan vocab · lifespan_band The lifespan band this section is paced for. A brief-band section covers the same ground at a quicker tempo; absent means the standard pace fits all comers.
session_duration duration How long one class session runs.
seat_capacity integer How many learners the section seats.

Constraints

RuleWhat it protects
course_stage_is_taught_at_school A course's stage is one its school actually teaches.
check school.stages_offered contains stage
course_band_is_taught_at_school A course meets in a band when its school actually runs classes.
check school.instruction_bands contains offered_band
