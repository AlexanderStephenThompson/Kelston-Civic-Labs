Field Station

kelston.science.field_station v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like FSTN-000482

A fixed place where an institute measures the world - a stream gauge hut, a canopy weather mast, a kelp-bed survey frame, the observatory dome itself. Stations are places, so the registry already knows their realm and tier; what this adds is who can physically work them.

Extends kelston.core.place — inherited fields appear below.
