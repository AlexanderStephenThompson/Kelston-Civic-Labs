Dwelling

kelston.core.dwelling v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like DWL-000482 · 1,890 records in town

A home. Dwellings carry the structural guarantees of the housing standard - clearance, water and flight access, burrow level, climate provision - as plain data, so "does this home fit this household" is a query, not a viewing.
