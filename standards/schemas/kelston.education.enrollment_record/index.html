Enrollment Record

kelston.education.enrollment_record v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like ENRL-000482

One citizen joining one course - the moment a learner takes a seat. Extends the civic record, so a citizen's whole education reads as one dated thread on the town's shared event spine.
