Agriculture & Food Systems

Market Lot

kelston.agriculture.market_lot v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like LOT-000482 · 225 records in town

A quantity of food offered for trade - fresh from a harvest or crafted as a product. The lot is where agriculture meets commerce, priced in kelmarks at a declared market realm.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
seller ref · organization required The organization offering the lot.
harvest ref · harvest_record The harvest this lot draws from, for fresh produce.
product ref · food_product The crafted product this lot offers, for made goods.
lot_mass quantity (kilogram) required Mass of food in the lot.
unit_price quantity (kelmark) required Asking price per kilogram, in kelmarks.
offered_on date required The day the lot was listed.
market_realm ref · realm required The realm whose market the lot is offered at.

Constraints

RuleWhat it protects
