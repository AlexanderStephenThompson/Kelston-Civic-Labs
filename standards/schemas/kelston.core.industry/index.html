Industry

kelston.core.industry v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like IND-000482 · 73 records in town

The middle level of the sector -> industry -> role taxonomy: a coherent line of work within a sector, e.g. "orcharding" within agriculture. Industries are data, not prose, so the taxonomy itself is versioned and queryable like everything else.
