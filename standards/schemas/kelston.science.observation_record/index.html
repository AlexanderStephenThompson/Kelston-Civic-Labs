Observation Record

kelston.science.observation_record v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like OBSV-000482

One observation taken at one field station - a reading, a sighting, a measurement. Extends the civic record, so the town's science sits on the same dated spine as its harvests and hearings, open for any citizen to query.

Extends kelston.core.civic_record — inherited fields appear below.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
field_station ref · field_station required The station where the observation was taken.
observed_at datetime required When the phenomenon itself was observed, distinct from when the record was filed.
phenomenon text required What was observed, in plain words - "first frost on the upper terraces".
air_temperature quantity (celsius) Air temperature at the station when the observation was taken, when measured.
is_anomalous boolean Whether the observation falls outside the station's expected range.
