Core standard

Appointment

kelston.core.appointment v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like APT-000482 · 2,226 records in town

A citizen holding a role at an organization for a span of time, on a declared shift band. The join record every employment question runs through - who works where, doing what, and when.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
citizen ref · citizen required The citizen holding the position.
role ref · role required The role being performed.
organization ref · organization required The organization where the role is held.
began_on date required First day in the position.
ended_on date Last day in the position; absent while the appointment is active.
shift_band vocab · shift_band required The shift band this appointment is worked in.

Constraints

RuleWhat it protects
role_matches_organization_sector The role's industry belongs to the employing organization's sector.
check role.industry.sector eq organization.sector
