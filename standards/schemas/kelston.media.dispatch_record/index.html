Dispatch Record

kelston.media.dispatch_record v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like DSPC-000482

One release of one piece of news through one channel - a chime pattern rung, a sheet posted, a beacon sequence shown. Extends the civic record, so what the town was told sits on the same event spine as what the town did.
