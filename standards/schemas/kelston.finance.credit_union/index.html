Finance & Mutual Credit

Credit Union

kelston.finance.credit_union v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like CRU-000482

A member-owned organization that keeps kelmark accounts, makes loans from its shared reserve, and stewards the mutual pools. Credit unions are organizations first, so the registry already knows their realm, industry, and hours.

Extends kelston.core.organization — inherited fields appear below.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
member_count integer required Number of citizens holding membership shares.
reserve_balance quantity (kelmark) required Kelmarks held in the union's common reserve.
member_lending_limit quantity (kelmark) required The most any one member may have on loan at once, in kelmarks.
includes_insurance_pools boolean Whether the union stewards mutual pools alongside its accounts.
