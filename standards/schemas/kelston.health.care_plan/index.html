Health & Wellness

Care Plan

kelston.health.care_plan v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like CARE-000482

One patient's ongoing course of care at one clinic - the thread that ties visits together. A plan exists whenever care runs longer than a single visit: a recovery, a pregnancy, a molt season, a hibernation watched over from autumn to spring.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
patient ref · citizen required The citizen this plan cares for.
clinic ref · clinic required The clinic carrying the plan.
lead_clinician ref · citizen The clinician who knows this patient best and steers the plan.
focus vocab · care_focus required What the plan is chiefly about, from preventive checkups to hibernation support.
opened_on date required The day the plan was opened.
closed_on date The day the plan was closed well; absent while care continues.
review_interval_duration duration How often the plan is reviewed, paced to the patient - a brief-band citizen's chronic plan is reviewed in weeks, a venerable tortoise's in seasons.
