Household

kelston.core.household v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like HSH-000482 · 1,890 records in town

The citizens who share one dwelling. Mixed-species households are the Kelston norm, which is exactly why housing guarantees are checked per member, never per household average.

FieldTypeReq.Description
dwelling ref · dwelling required The home this household lives in.

members list of ref · citizen required Every citizen who lives in this dwelling.
