Species Profile

kelston.core.species_profile v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like SPC-000482 · 58 records in town

The structural trait standard for one species of citizen. Every system in town - housing, scheduling, transit, employment, care - reads traits from here rather than guessing, which is how Kelston serves a shrew and a giraffe with the same schemas.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
common_name text required The everyday name of the species, e.g. "red fox".
animal_class vocab · animal_class required Which citizen family the species belongs to - mammal, reptile, or bird.
family_group text required Informal family grouping used for naming pools and kinship flavor, e.g. "canine", "raptor", "tortoise".
size_class vocab · size_class required Body-size band driving clearance, seating, and equipment standards.
activity_cycle vocab · activity_cycle required When the species is naturally awake and at its best.
locomotion list of vocab · locomotion required All movement modes the species is fully at home in.
thermoregulation vocab · thermoregulation required Endotherm or ectotherm; drives climate provisions everywhere.
lifespan_band vocab · lifespan_band required Expected lifespan band, used to pace lifelong services fairly.
habitat_affinity list of vocab · realm_code Realms the species tends to settle in. Affinity weights distributions; it never restricts where a citizen may live.

Constraints

RuleWhat it protects
reptiles_are_ectotherms In Kelston's roster, reptile-class species are the ectotherms.
when animal_class eq reptile · check thermoregulation eq ectotherm
warm_classes_are_endotherms Mammal- and bird-class species regulate their own heat.
when animal_class in ['mammal', 'bird'] · check thermoregulation eq endotherm
