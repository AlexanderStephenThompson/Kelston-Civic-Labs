Health & Wellness

Clinic

kelston.health.clinic v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like CLN-000482

An organization that gives medical care - a neighborhood surgery, the realm infirmary, a traveling field clinic's home base. Clinics are organizations first; what this adds is who the rooms can actually serve, because a clinic that cannot fit or warm its patient is not a clinic for that patient.

Extends kelston.core.organization — inherited fields appear below.
