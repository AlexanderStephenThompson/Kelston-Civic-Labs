Finance & Mutual Credit

Kelmark Ledger Entry

kelston.finance.ledger_entry v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like LGR-000482

One movement of kelmarks on the credit union's books - a deposit, a loan, a pool contribution or payout. Extends the civic record, so the town's money sits on the same event spine as everything else it does.

Extends kelston.core.civic_record — inherited fields appear below.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
union ref · credit_union required The credit union whose books carry the entry.
member ref · citizen required The member whose account the entry moves.
entry_kind vocab · kelmark_entry_kind required What kind of movement this entry records.
amount quantity (kelmark) required Kelmarks moved by the entry, always a positive number.
pool ref · mutual_pool The mutual pool involved, for contribution and payout entries.

Constraints

RuleWhat it protects
