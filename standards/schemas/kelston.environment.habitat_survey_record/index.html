Habitat Survey Record

kelston.environment.habitat_survey_record v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like HSURV-000482

One walked (or swum, or flown) survey of one reserve - the moment a habitat's condition becomes a number the town can act on. Surveys sit on the civic record spine like every other dated record.

Extends kelston.core.civic_record — inherited fields appear below.
