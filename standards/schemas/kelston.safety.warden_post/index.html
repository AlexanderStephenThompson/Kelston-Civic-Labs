Warden Post

kelston.safety.warden_post v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like WPOST-000482

An organization of wardens responsible for safety in a stretch of town - patrol, rescue, and the first calm voice in a dispute. A post's coverage and specialties are data, so "who answers here, in this band, in this water" is a query, not a hope.
