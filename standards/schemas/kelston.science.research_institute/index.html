Research Institute

kelston.science.research_institute v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like RSRCH-000482

An organization that studies the town and its world - the observatory, the soils lab, the standards research bureau. Institutes are organizations first; what this adds is what they study and the open promise that what they learn belongs to everyone.
