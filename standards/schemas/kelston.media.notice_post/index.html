Communication & Media

Notice Post

kelston.media.notice_post v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like NPST-000482

A fixed point where one channel reaches the public - a chime tower on the square, a print board by the tram stop, a beacon lamp on a rooftop, a drum station in the under-market. Posts are places, so every citizen can find the nearest one in their own tier.

Extends kelston.core.place — inherited fields appear below.
