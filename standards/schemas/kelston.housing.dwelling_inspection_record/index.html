Dwelling Inspection Record

kelston.housing.dwelling_inspection_record v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like HINSP-000482

One inspection of one home against the housing standard - the moment the promises a dwelling makes on paper (clearance, water and flight access, climate provision) are checked against the home as built.

Extends kelston.core.civic_record — inherited fields appear below.

Fields

is_climate_provision_verified boolean required Whether the home's declared climate provision was found working.
