Health & Wellness

Clinic Visit

kelston.health.clinic_visit v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like CLNV-000482

One patient seen once at one clinic - the basic unit of town medicine. Extends the civic record, so visits sit on the same event spine as every other dated record, and the species-fit guarantees are checked right here where patient meets room.

Extends kelston.core.civic_record — inherited fields appear below.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
patient ref · citizen required The citizen who was seen.
clinic ref · clinic required The clinic where the visit happened.
care_plan ref · care_plan The ongoing care plan this visit belongs to, when there is one.
visited_on date required The day of the visit.
visit_band vocab · shift_band The shift band the visit was booked in. Scheduling follows the patient's preferred band, so a nocturnal patient is never asked to be examined half-asleep at noon.
body_mass quantity (kilogram) The patient's mass at check-in, the one vital every species shares.
body_temperature quantity (celsius) Body temperature at check-in, read against the species' own normal range.
follow_up_due_on date When the patient should next be seen; absent if no follow-up is needed.

when patient.species.thermoregulation eq ectotherm · check clinic.has_basking_recovery eq True
