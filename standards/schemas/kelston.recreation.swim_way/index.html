Swim-Way

kelston.recreation.swim_way v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like SWIM-000482

A kept channel of open water for recreation - a marked river run, a shoreline lap lane, a still pond loop. Swim-ways declare their depth and the largest swimmer they admit, so an otter pup and a crocodile elder each find water that fits.
