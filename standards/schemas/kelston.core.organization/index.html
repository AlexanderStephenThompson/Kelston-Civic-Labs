Core standard

Organization

kelston.core.organization v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like ORG-000482 · 296 records in town

Any collective actor in town - a farm, a clinic, a guild school, the registry itself. Organizations declare their sector, industry, realm, and operating cycle so the whole economy is one queryable surface.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
name text required The organization's registered name.
sector vocab · sector required The sector this organization works in.
industry ref · industry required The industry, from its sector's taxonomy.
realm ref · realm required The realm where the organization is based.
operating_cycle vocab · operating_cycle required When the organization is open and staffed.
founded_on date The date the organization was registered.

Constraints

RuleWhat it protects
industry_belongs_to_sector An organization's industry comes from its own sector's taxonomy.
check industry.sector eq sector
