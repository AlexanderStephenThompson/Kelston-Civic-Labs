Works Site

kelston.utilities.works_site v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like WKSITE-000482

A located installation where a utility works does its work - a solar terrace, a mill race wheelhouse, a pumphouse, a composting yard. Sites are the unit at which output, capacity, and crew needs are recorded.

Extends kelston.core.place — inherited fields appear below.
