Parks Trust

kelston.recreation.parks_trust v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like TRST-000482

An organization that keeps recreation grounds for everyone - the commons greens, the swim-ways, the climbing groves, the glide parks. Trusts are organizations first, so the registry already knows their realm, staff, and hours.
