Performance Company

kelston.arts.performance_company v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like PERF-000482

An organization that makes and shows art - a theater troupe, a swim ballet, a storytelling circle, a festival craft guild. Companies are organizations first, so the registry already knows their realm, hours, and people.
