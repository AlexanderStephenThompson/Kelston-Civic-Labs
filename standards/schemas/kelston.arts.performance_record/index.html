Performance Record

kelston.arts.performance_record v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like SHOW-000482

One staging of one work before an audience - the unit at which the town's cultural life is counted. Extends the civic record, so a premiere sits on the same event spine as a harvest or a ledger entry.
