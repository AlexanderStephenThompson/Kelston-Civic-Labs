Core standard

Place

kelston.core.place v1.0.0 · stable · abstract (no instances; exists to be extended)

The abstract base for anything with a location in Kelston: parcels, dwellings, growing plots, market halls. A place knows its realm and its vertical tier; everything else is for the kinds that extend it.

FieldTypeReq.Description
