Mobility & Transit

Transit Stop

kelston.mobility.transit_stop v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like STOP-000482

A named place where citizens board and leave a transit service - a tram halt, a ferry landing, a glideway perch station, a burrow shuttle mouth. A stop's accessibility is plain data, so route planning can promise a citizen every stop on the way actually fits them.

Extends kelston.core.place — inherited fields appear below.
