Grounds Patrol Record

kelston.recreation.patrol_record v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like PTRL-000482

One watch round over one recreation ground - a swim-way swept, a climbing grove checked, a night green walked. Extends the civic record, so the town's care for its grounds sits on the same event spine as everything else it does.

Extends kelston.core.civic_record — inherited fields appear below.
