Governance & Civic Registry

Civic Office

kelston.governance.civic_office v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like CVOF-000482

An organization that does the town's public business - the registry of record, the assembly secretariat, the standards office. Offices are organizations first, so staffing and registry queries already understand them; what this adds is jurisdiction and the promise of a staffed counter.

Extends kelston.core.organization — inherited fields appear below.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
mandate text required One plain sentence on what this office is charged to do.
jurisdiction_realms list of ref · realm required The realms this office serves; every realm appears in some office's list.
public_counter_bands list of vocab · shift_band required The shift bands when the public counter is staffed. Offices spread their counters across bands so a nocturnal citizen is served in their own hours, not asked to fight their biology for a permit.
is_registry_authority boolean Whether this office may issue and amend records of the civic registry.
