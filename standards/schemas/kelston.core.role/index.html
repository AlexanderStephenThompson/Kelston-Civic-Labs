Role

kelston.core.role v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like ROL-000482 · 148 records in town

A position definition in the taxonomy - what the work is, which industry it belongs to, and what capabilities it genuinely requires. Requirements are explicit data so matching is fair: a role demands flight only when the work truly happens in the air.
