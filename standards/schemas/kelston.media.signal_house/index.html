Communication & Media

Signal House

kelston.media.signal_house v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like SGNL-000482

An organization that publishes to the town - the town signal itself, a realm print works, or a courier post office. Signal houses declare the channels they publish through, because in Kelston a notice that only reaches one sensorium has not been published at all.

Extends kelston.core.organization — inherited fields appear below.
