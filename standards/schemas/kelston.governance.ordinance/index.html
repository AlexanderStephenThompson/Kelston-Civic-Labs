Governance & Civic Registry

Ordinance

kelston.governance.ordinance v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like ORDN-000482

A binding town standard adopted by the assembly - the housing guarantee, the climate provision rules, the market weights. An ordinance exists from the day it is proposed, and its whole life is queryable data.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
name text required The ordinance's short citable name, e.g. "The Clearance Standard".
summary text required One or two plain sentences on what the ordinance requires.
stewarding_office ref · civic_office required The civic office responsible for keeping this ordinance current.
lifecycle_stage vocab · ordinance_status required Where the ordinance stands in its life, from proposed to retired.
adopted_on date The day the assembly voted the ordinance in; absent while proposed.
applies_to_realms list of ref · realm Realms where the ordinance applies; an empty list means town-wide.
review_due_on date When the stewarding office must next review whether the ordinance still fits life.

Constraints

RuleWhat it protects
binding_ordinances_have_adoption_date An ordinance that binds anyone records the day it was voted in.
when lifecycle_stage in ['adopted', 'amended'] · check adopted_on present
