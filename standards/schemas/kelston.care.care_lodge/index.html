Social Services & Care

Care Lodge

kelston.care.care_lodge v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like CLDG-000482

An organization that gives daily support - an elder lodge, an early-years day den, a respite house, a mutual-aid hall. Lodges are organizations first; what this adds is who the house can truly hold and keep comfortable, because care begins with a building that fits.

Extends kelston.core.organization — inherited fields appear below.
