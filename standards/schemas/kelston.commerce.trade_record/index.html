Trade Record

kelston.commerce.trade_record v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like TRD-000482

One completed exchange at a market stall - goods handed over, kelmarks settled. Extends the civic record, so every trade sits on the same event spine as harvests, permits, and the rest of the town's day.
