Finance & Mutual Credit

Mutual Pool

kelston.finance.mutual_pool v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like POOL-000482

A shared kelmark fund that members pay into regularly and draw from in need - storm repair, lean-season bridging, care costs. A pool exists from the day its charter is signed at a credit union.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
steward_union ref · credit_union required The credit union that stewards the pool's books.
purpose text required What the pool exists to cover, in plain words.
chartered_on date required The day the pool's charter was signed.
pooled_balance quantity (kelmark) required Kelmarks currently held by the pool.
contribution_amount quantity (kelmark) required The regular share each member pays in, in kelmarks.
payout_ceiling quantity (kelmark) required The most a single payout may draw, in kelmarks.

Constraints

RuleWhat it protects
payout_ceiling_within_balance A pool never promises a single payout larger than what it holds.
check payout_ceiling lte pooled_balance
