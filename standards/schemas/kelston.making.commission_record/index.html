Commission Record

kelston.making.commission_record v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like CMSN-000482

One agreement for one made thing - a citizen asks, a workshop agrees, a price and a promise date are set. Extends the civic record, so the town's making sits on the same event spine as its growing and trading.
