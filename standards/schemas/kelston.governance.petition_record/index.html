Petition Record

kelston.governance.petition_record v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like PETN-000482

One citizen request to the town - a new crossing, an amended standard, a counter kept open later. Extends the civic record, so petitions sit on the same event spine as everything else, and every petition gets a recorded answer.

Extends kelston.core.civic_record — inherited fields appear below.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
petitioner ref · citizen required The citizen who brought the petition.
subject text required One sentence stating what is being asked for.
related_ordinance ref · ordinance The ordinance the petition asks to change or invoke, when there is one.
submitted_on date required The day the petition was logged at the counter.
heard_in_band vocab · shift_band The shift band the hearing is scheduled in. Hearings follow the petitioner's preferred band - the town comes to the citizen's hours.
outcome vocab · petition_outcome required How the petition fared; updated as it moves through the process.
decided_on date The day a decision was reached; absent until then.
