Support Visit Record

kelston.care.support_visit_record v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like SUPV-000482

One care-circle visit to its focus citizen, with what was actually done. Extends the civic record, so the quiet daily work of mutual aid is as visible on the town's event spine as any harvest or assembly vote.

Extends kelston.core.civic_record — inherited fields appear below.
