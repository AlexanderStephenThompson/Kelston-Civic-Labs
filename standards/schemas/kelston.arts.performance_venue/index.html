Performance Venue

kelston.arts.performance_venue v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like VNUE-000482

A place built for showing art - a commons amphitheater, a canopy stage, a shoreline hall with a submerged gallery. Venues declare what bodies they seat, because a show no grand-class neighbor can attend is only half a show.
