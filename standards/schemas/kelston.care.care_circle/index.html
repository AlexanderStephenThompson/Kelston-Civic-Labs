Social Services & Care

Care Circle

kelston.care.care_circle v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like CCIR-000482

A small standing group of citizens organized around one neighbor who needs support - the signature unit of Kelston mutual aid. A circle exists from the day neighbors agree to share the looking-after, whether or not a lodge ever gets involved.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
focus_citizen ref · citizen required The citizen the circle gathers around.
coordinating_lodge ref · care_lodge The care lodge backing the circle, when one does.
formed_on date required The day the circle agreed to form.
support_kinds list of vocab · care_support required The kinds of support the circle provides, from shared meals to basking watch.
gathering_band vocab · shift_band The shift band the circle gathers and visits in - always the focus citizen's own preferred band, because care comes on their hours.

Constraints

RuleWhat it protects
circle_gathers_on_focus_citizens_hours When a circle sets a gathering band, it is the focus citizen's preferred band.
when gathering_band present · check gathering_band eq focus_citizen.active_hours_preference
