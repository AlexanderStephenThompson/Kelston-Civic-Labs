Core standard

Citizen

kelston.core.citizen v1.0.0 · stable · ids look like CTZ-000482 · 5,000 records in town

A resident of Kelston. The citizen record is the spine of the civic registry: every sector's records reach a citizen by this id, and the housing and scheduling guarantees are checked right here, where the citizen meets their home and their hours.

Fields

FieldTypeReq.Description
given_name text required The citizen's personal name.
family_name text required The citizen's family or lodge name.
species ref · species_profile required The species profile whose structural traits apply to this citizen.
born_on date required Date of birth in the civic calendar.
household ref · household required The household this citizen belongs to.
realm ref · realm required The realm of residence.
active_hours_preference vocab · shift_band required The shift band this citizen prefers to be active in. Defaults follow the species activity cycle, but the preference is the citizen's own - owls with day jobs exist.

Constraints

RuleWhat it protects
home_fits_citizen A citizen's dwelling clearance must meet their size class.
check household.dwelling.clearance_class gte species.size_class
residence_realm_matches_home The realm of residence is the realm the dwelling stands in.
check realm eq household.dwelling.realm
semi_aquatic_homes_have_water_access Semi-aquatic citizens live in homes with swim-in access.
when species.locomotion contains semi_aquatic · check household.dwelling.has_water_access eq True
ectotherm_homes_keep_warm Ectotherm citizens get basking provisions at home, unless they live in the Drylands where the realm itself provides.
when species.thermoregulation eq ectotherm · python:core.ectotherm_home_is_warm_enough
