kelston.vocab.climate_provision

v1.0.0 · 4 terms

What a dwelling or workplace provides beyond ambient conditions. The climate standard guarantees ectotherms basking heat and heavy-coat citizens cold comfort - provisions are data, so the guarantee is checkable.
