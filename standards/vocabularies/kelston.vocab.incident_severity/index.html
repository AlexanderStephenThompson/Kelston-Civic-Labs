v1.0.0 · ordered (term order is meaningful - constraints may compare with ≥/≤) · 4 terms

How serious an incident is, from minor to critical. Ordered, so dispatch rules and reviews can compare severities mechanically.

Terms

#CodeLabelDescription
1 minor Minor Handled on the spot with no lasting harm.
2 moderate Moderate Needed a response crew but resolved within the shift.
3 serious Serious Injury, significant damage, or a multi-crew response.
4 critical Critical Life-threatening or town-scale; the whole post turns out.
