kelston.vocab.rescue_specialty

v1.0.0 · 6 terms

The rescue disciplines a warden post or rescue station can field. Each specialty maps to real capabilities - water rescue needs swimmers, aerial rescue needs flight - so coverage is checkable, not assumed.
