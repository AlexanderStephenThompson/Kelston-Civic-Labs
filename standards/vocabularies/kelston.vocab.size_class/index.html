kelston.vocab.size_class

v1.0.0 · ordered (term order is meaningful - constraints may compare with ≥/≤) · 5 terms

Body-size bands that drive housing clearance, transit seating, furniture standards, and equipment sizing across every sector. Ordered, so systems can check "fits" mechanically: a dwelling's clearance_class must be at least every occupant's size_class.

Terms

#CodeLabelDescription
1 tiny Tiny Under about 25 cm standing height - mice, geckos, wrens. Uses micro-scale fixtures.
2 small Small Roughly 25 cm to 1 m - rabbits, foxes, ravens, monitor lizards.
3 medium Medium Roughly 1 m to 2 m - wolves, deer, capybaras, emus.
4 large Large Roughly 2 m to 3.5 m - bears, big cats, crocodiles, ostriches.
5 grand Grand Above 3.5 m - elephants, giraffes, moose. Needs grand-clearance architecture.
