kelston.vocab.land_use

v1.0.0 · 7 terms

What a parcel of Kelston ground (or water, or canopy) is dedicated to. One parcel, one primary use; mixed-use is its own honest category.

Terms

CodeLabelDescription
residential Residential Dwellings and their gardens.
agricultural Agricultural Farms, commons gardens, orchards, paddies, and apiaries.
civic Civic Registry halls, schools, clinics, and assembly grounds.
commercial Commercial Shops, market halls, and workshops that trade with the public.
industrial Industrial Works yards, mills, and the composting and energy sites.
parkland Parkland Commons, groves, swim-ways, and protected wild edges.
mixed Mixed use Deliberately layered uses - shopfront below, dwellings above.
