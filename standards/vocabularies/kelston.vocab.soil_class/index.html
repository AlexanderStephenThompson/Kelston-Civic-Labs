Vocabulary

kelston.vocab.soil_class

v1.0.0 · 7 terms

Dominant soil classification of a growing plot, used for crop matching and soil-care planning. Kelston's classes are deliberately few and plain; the soils lab keeps the fine detail in survey records.

Terms

CodeLabelDescription
loam Loam Balanced, fertile soil - the Evergreen and Commons standard.
clay Clay Heavy, water-holding soil common along the Shorelines flats.
sand Sand Fast-draining soil of the Drylands; thrives under drip irrigation.
silt Silt Fine river-laid soil of the floodplain plots.
peat Peat Rich wetland soil at the Shorelines and Frostlands margins.
volcanic Volcanic Dark mineral soil of the Highlands terraces.
substrate_water Water substrate No soil at all - submerged paddies, kelp beds, and aquaponic rafts.
