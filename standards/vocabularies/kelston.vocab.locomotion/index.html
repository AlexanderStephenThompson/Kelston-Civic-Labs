kelston.vocab.locomotion

v1.0.0 · 5 terms

How a species gets around. Species carry one or more modes; mobility, architecture, and role requirements all key off this standard.

Terms

CodeLabelDescription
terrestrial Terrestrial Moves on the ground. Nearly universal baseline mode.
arboreal Arboreal At home climbing and living in trees and vertical structure.
flighted Flighted True flight - most birds and the bats. Unlocks aerial lanes and aerie housing.
semi_aquatic Semi-aquatic Swims as readily as walks - otters, seals, turtles, crocodiles, seabirds.
fossorial Fossorial Digs and lives comfortably underground - moles, badgers, burrowing owls.
