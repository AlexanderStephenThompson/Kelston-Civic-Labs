kelston.vocab.habitat_condition

v1.0.0 · ordered (term order is meaningful - constraints may compare with ≥/≤) · 4 terms

How a surveyed habitat is faring, from failing to thriving. Ordered, so stewardship plans can compare surveys mechanically - a reserve that drops a band triggers a follow-up by rule, not by memory.

Terms

#CodeLabelDescription
1 failing Failing Core habitat function is breaking down; immediate intervention needed.
2 strained Strained Under visible pressure - declining cover, water, or wild residents.
3 stable Stable Holding steady; routine stewardship is enough.
4 thriving Thriving Rich, regenerating, and full of wild residents.
