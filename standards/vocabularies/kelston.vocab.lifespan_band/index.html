kelston.vocab.lifespan_band

v1.0.0 · ordered (term order is meaningful - constraints may compare with ≥/≤) · 4 terms

Expected lifespan bands. Care services, pensions, and education pacing are lifespan-aware so a brief-band shrew and a venerable-band tortoise both get a whole life's worth of civic services at their own tempo.

Terms

#CodeLabelDescription

2 standard Standard Roughly 15 to 40 years - most mid-sized mammals.
3 long Long Roughly 40 to 80 years - elephants, large raptors, crocodilians.
4 venerable Venerable Beyond 80 years - tortoises and other famously patient citizens.
