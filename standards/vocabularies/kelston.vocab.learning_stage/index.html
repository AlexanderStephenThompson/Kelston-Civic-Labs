kelston.vocab.learning_stage

v1.0.0 · ordered (term order is meaningful - constraints may compare with ≥/≤) · 5 terms

The stages of a Kelston education, from first words to lifelong study. Ordered, so systems can check progression mechanically - a guild apprenticeship sits above the lyceum it builds on.

Terms

#CodeLabelDescription
1 early_years Early years First learning - play, language, and getting along across species.
2 primary Primary The shared foundation every citizen receives - letters, numbers, civics.
3 lyceum Lyceum Broad upper study before a citizen chooses a working path.
4 guild_apprenticeship Guild apprenticeship Trade study under a guild school, learned at the bench and in the field.
5 lifelong Lifelong Open courses any citizen may join at any age, in any band.
