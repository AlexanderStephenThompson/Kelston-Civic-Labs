kelston.vocab.realm_code

v1.0.0 · 7 terms

Kelston's seven realms, each a biome with its own character, agriculture, and architecture. Every realm is mixed-species; affinity only weights who tends to settle where.

commons The Commons The central mixed urban hub - civic life, markets, and the registry.

canopy The Canopy Jungle realm - vertical living where arboreal and flighted citizens thrive.
frostlands The Frostlands Tundra realm - greenhouse agriculture and heavy-coat neighborhoods.
shorelines The Shorelines Coastal realm - harbors, aquaculture, and semi-aquatic life.
highlands The Highlands Mountain realm - terraced slopes, aeries, and thin-air specialists.
drylands The Drylands Desert realm - irrigated dryland farming, basking grounds, dusk-active culture.
