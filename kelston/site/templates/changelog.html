The Standards Office

How a standard changes in Kelston

Every standard is semver'd, checksummed, and historied in the registry manifest. Changes are classified mechanically: PATCH touches only prose, MINOR adds things every record can adopt by re-validation, and MAJOR ships with a migration - or the registry refuses it.

The worked example: growing plot 2.0.0

On 2026-06-10 the standards office split the growing plot's combined 1.x soil codes into texture (soil_class) plus water behavior (drainage_class), and renamed keeper to steward, the town-wide word. A MAJOR change - so it shipped with a declarative migration, and one command brought every plot in town to the new standard, re-validated: 

$ kelston upgrade
