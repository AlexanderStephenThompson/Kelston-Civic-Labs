Universal: Data, infrastructure, marketing
Get
Extract / harvest raw inputs
•	Fossil energy: oil, gas, coal 
•	Renewable energy: solar, wind, hydro, geothermal capture 
•	Metals & minerals: ore, rare earths, stone, sand, salt 
•	Biological: agriculture, livestock, fishing/aquaculture, forestry (Crop & Chop's first move lives here) 
•	Water: freshwater capture 
•	Reclaimed: scrap, recycling, materials recovery. Getting raw inputs from the waste stream instead of the ground.
Make
Process / manufacture / build
•	Materials: refining, smelting, chemicals, milling, packaging stock. The stuff other things get made from. 
•	Components & equipment: semiconductors, parts, machinery, industrial equipment. 
•	Durable goods: vehicles, electronics, appliances, furniture, aerospace & defense. 
•	Consumables: food & beverage, pharma, personal & household products, tobacco. 
•	Structures: construction & engineering. The only "make" built in place rather than in a factory. 
•	Power generation (seam item): turning fuel or sunlight into electricity. It's a make (a transformation), even though the grid that carries it is a move.
•	Digital Development: media production, game dev, software* (intangible goods)
Move
Distribute / transmit / sell-through
•	Freight & logistics: trucking, rail, air cargo, shipping. 
•	Storage & warehousing (the one nobody lists): the pause inside move; holding inventory between hops. 
•	Energy transmission: pipelines, electric grid, gas distribution. 
•	Utilities distribution: water, waste. 
•	Digital transmission: telecom, broadband. Moving bits instead of atoms. 
•	Retail / last-mile (seam): wholesale, retail, e-commerce fulfillment. This is the doorway into Phase 2: where "move" hands off to "who uses it."
