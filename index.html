The Town of Kelston

A town that records things well only has to solve each problem once.

Kelston is a thought experiment made real: a town of 5,000 animal citizens across seven biome realms, run on data standards designed to be understood at first read. Everything on this site is generated from the town's actual records - the same ones its validator proves.

The standards

Every field, industry, and role in town is defined up front, in 68 schemas and 38 shared vocabularies maintained by the standards office. Names read as sentences, every field carries a description, and updates reach every record in town through one command.
