Geography

The seven realms

Kelston grew where seven biomes meet. Every realm is mixed-species - species affinity weights who tends to settle where, never who may - and each gives the town a distinct architecture, agriculture, and rhythm.

The Canopy

A vertical realm of platforms, rope-ways, and flowering dark understory, where most front doors are forty meters up.

dense jungle and rainforest · 650 residents

The Commons

The central hub where the realms meet - the registry of record, the assembly hall, the grand market, and the densest mixed-species streets.

temperate meadow and river junction · 1,300 residents

The Drylands

Drip-fed groves, shaded courtyards, public basking grounds, and the night markets that wake when the other realms sleep.

desert and scrubland · 650 residents

The Evergreen

Timbercraft and orchard country; trunk lodges above and warm burrow streets below, with berry rows in every clearing.

temperate conifer and broadleaf forest · 750 residents

The Frostlands

The glass quarter - long lights of greenhouse rows against the snow, and the warmest indoor culture in town.

tundra and snowfield · 450 residents

The Highlands

Terraced stone villages under the aeries; thin air, long views, and the observatory at the summit road's end.

mountain slopes and alpine meadow · 500 residents

The Shorelines

Harbors, stilt streets, and the paddies and kelp lines that feed half the town; the only realm with submerged front rooms.

coast, estuary, and tidal flats · 700 residents
